OUR STORY

Crafted with Fire,
Served with Love.

Since 2018, STACKLY has been on a simple mission: to serve honest, handcrafted pizza that brings people together. We started with a single wood-fired oven in Besant Nagar, and today, we're proud to be Chennai's favourite neighborhood pizzeria.

We still hand-stretch every dough, source our tomatoes locally, and bake each slice until it's perfect. Because to us, pizza isn't just food — it's a craft.
